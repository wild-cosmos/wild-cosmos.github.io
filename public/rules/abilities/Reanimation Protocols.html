When this model is destroyed, place a Reanimation marker on as close as possible to their last position, and roll a D4 to determine when they will reanimate:

D4 Reanimation
2+ This model reanimates during this round.
1 This model reanimates during the next round.
0 This model does not reanimate - remove the marker.

Models reanimate during the Recovery step of the Resolution Phase. When a model reanimates, remove their Reanimation marker and set this model up again as close as possible to that position, and more than 1" from any enemy models. The reanimating model regains 1 life, and can then use their Living Metal ability. In the Morale step of the Resolution Phase, a model that reanimated during that round automatically falters (they should still roll for the morale check if there are any other effects they could suffer, such as panicking).

Every time a model rolls on the Reanimation Table, subtract 1 from the result for each previous time they have reanimated during that game (to a minimum of 0).

If a destroyed model with this ability was part of a unit, they are no longer part of that unit, unless they move or reanimate within 2" of the unit, in which case they immediately rejoin it. They cannot join a different unit.
