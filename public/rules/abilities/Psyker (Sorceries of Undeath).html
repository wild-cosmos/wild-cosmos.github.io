If any Psykers in your warband know powers from this psychic discipline, you may also include one of the following Apparitions in your warband: Mortalis Terminexus, Shyish Reaper, Suffocating Gravetide, Spirit Hosts or Vault of Souls.

Psychis Power ✷ Description
Eerie 1 Blessing. Select a visible friendly Ethereal model or unit that is either on the battlefield or in reserve. If they are on the battlefield remove them; and then set them up wholly within 6" of the Psyker. They must be set up more than 1" away from any enemy models, unless the Psyker is within 1" of any enemy models - if they are set up within 1" of any enemy models, this does not count as a charge. The Mv value of this model or unit is reduced to 0" until the end of the phase, but they are treated as having moved their full ordinary Mv value for all purposes (for example, they cannot aim when attacking with ranged weapons). The cost to manifest this power increases by 1✷ for each of the following that applies: the affected models are a unit, Large or Heavy Support.
Ghastly Procession 1 Blessing. This power affects the Psyker. Until the end of the round, visible friendly Malignants within 9" of the Psyker add 3" to their Mv value. 1✷: This power affects visible friendly Malignants within 18". 2✷: Visible friendly Malignants that are within 5" of the Psyker when this power is manifested fight first in the Close Quarters Phase this round if they charged in the Manoeuvring Phase.
Fade to Shadow 1 Blessing. Select a visible friendly model or unit within 12" of the Psyker. Until the end of the round, the selected models have the Stealth (1) ability. ?✷: Improve the value of this Stealth ability by an amount equal to the additional ✷ spent on this power.
Soul Cage 2 Hex. Select a visible enemy model within 9" of the psyker. Until the end of the round, that model cannot move, and they incur a hit roll penalty of 1 for all of their melee attacks. ?✷: Increase the hit roll penalty by an amount equal to the additional ✷ spent on this power.
Spectural Tether 2 Blessing. Select a visible friendly Undead model within 12" of the Psyker. They have the Ignore Damage (6+) ability until the end of the round. ?✷: Improve the value of this ability by an amount equal to the additional ✷ spent on this power.
Spirti Siphon 1 Warpfire. Select a visible enemy model or unit within 18" of the Psyker: they suffer D2 AP 4 hits. For each injury inflicted by this power (that is not immediately discarded, for example by the Ignore Damage ability), the Psyker may discard one of their own injuries. If the Psyker does not have any injuries to discard, they instead discard the next injury they suffer. 1✷: Resolve each hit with Dm D2; or 2✷: Resolve each hit with Dm 2.
Touch of Death 1 Blessing. Select a visible friendly model within 12" of the Psyker. Until the end of the round, the affected model incurs a critical hit bonus of 1 for all of their melee attacks. ?✷: Increase the critical hit bonus by an amount equal to the additional ✷ spent on this power.
Vortex of Grief 2 Warpfire. Choose a point on the battlefield that is within 18" of the Psyker and visible to them. Each enemy model that is within 3" of this point suffers D2 AP 0 hits, and each enemy model that is within 6" of this point must make a Ld check: if the check is failed, that model cannot move any further in the Manoeuvring Phase that round, and they fight last in the Close Quarters Phase that round. This power cannot be manifested if the Psyker is within 1" of any enemy models. 2✷: Any model that makes a Ld check for this power must roll an extra dice and discard the highest result.
