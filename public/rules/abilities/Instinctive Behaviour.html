At the beginning of each round, this model must make a Ld check. If the check is failed, they suffer the following effects until the end of the round:

  • This model can only target the nearest visible enemy model or unit with any ranged attacks they make, unless they are firing defensively. If two models or units are equally close, you can choose which is targeted.
  • If this model is not armed with any ranged weapons, they must advance whenever possible, and must finish any move they make as close as possible to the enemy model or unit that was nearest to them and visible before they moved.
  • If the value rolled for the Ld check is less than half their Ld value (if the model is part of a unit, make this comparison against the Ld value used for the check), then until the end of the round this model is non-scoring.

If this model is faltering or panicking at the beginning of a round, they automatically fail the Ld check for Instinctive Behaviour.
