• This model is non-scoring, and their destruction is ignored for the purpose of scoring victory points.
  • This model cannot panic.
  • This model does not roll on the Malfunction Table when they are seriously injured, and instead suffers the normal penalties for being seriously injured.
  • This model does not count towards either the starting or current number of models in the warband when determining if the warband is broken.
  • If this model is not part of a unit, or they are in a unit that only contains other AI Support models, they may join another friendly unit by moving within 2" of them. They cannot join a unit if this would exceed the mormal limits on the number of models that can be in a unit.
  • If the only models in a warband remaining on the battlefield are AI Support models, and none of them are armed with weapons, they are immediately destroyed.
