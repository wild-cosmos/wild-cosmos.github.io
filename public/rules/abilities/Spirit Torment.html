Visible friendly Malignants within 9" of this model can use this model’s Ld value instead of their own. When a visible friendly Malignant within 9" of this model makes an attack, before they make any hit rolls, this model can threaten them with the judgement of Nagash: the threatened model re-rolls hit rolls of 1 until the end of the round, and must make a morale check in the following Resolution Phase.
