At the beginning of each round, this model gets D3 Nanoscarab tokens. At the end of any phase, or immediately before they are destroyed, this model may discard any number of their Nanoscarab tokens. For each Nanoscarab token discarded, choose one of the following:

  • Nominate a friendly Canoptek Scarabs model within 3″ of this model and roll a D8: on a 2+, they regain 1 life.
  • Nominate an enemy model within 3″ of this model and roll a D8: on a 2+, they suffer one AP 1 hit; on a 7+, they suffer one AP 4 hit instead.
