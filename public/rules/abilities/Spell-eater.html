Each time a visible model or unit within 9" of this model is targeted by a psychic power, this model can attempt to consume the power. Roll a D8: on a 5+, if the chosen power had been augmented, the effects of the augmentation are ignored; if the power had not been augmented, the effects of the power itself are ignored. For each power this model successfully consumes, they incur a critical hit bonus of 1 for attacks made with their chill dagger for the remainder of the game. No more than one model can attempt to consume each psychic power.
