At the beginning of each round, select which Canticle of the Omnissiah from the table below is in effect for the duration of that round: every model in your warband with this ability gains the benefit of that effect. No Canticle can be selected more than once during a game.

Alternatively, you can randomly determine which Canticle of the Omnissiah is in effect by rolling a D4 and consulting the table below. If you randomly determine a Canticle, it takes effect even if the same Canticle has been in effect earlier in the game.

D4Canticle
1 Benediction of the Omnissiah: Affected models re-roll hit rolls of 1 for ranged attacks.
2 Chant of the Remorseless Fist: Affected models re-roll hit rolls of 1 for melee attacks.
3 War Hymnal of Fortitude: Affected models re-roll 1s for their Ignore Damage ability.
4 Incantation of the Iron Soul: Affected models re-roll 1s for Ld checks.
Catechism of the Divine Chorus: Choose one of the Canticles above − this Canticle has the same effect.
