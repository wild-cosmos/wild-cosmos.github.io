Enemy models must re-roll successful hit rolls for attacks that target this model. The hit rolls that must be re-rolled are given in brackets next to this ability: for example, if the ability is Quick Reflexes (7+), successful hit rolls of 5+ must be re-rolled.

If a model has more than one Quick Reflexes ability, they only gain the benefit of the best of these abilities. Units can only benefit from Quick Reflexes if every model in the unit has a Quick Reflexes ability, and they only gain the benefit of the least effective version of the ability that any model in the unit is able to use.

Various ‘Reflexes’ abilities appear on different datasheets: Lightning Reflexes, Uncanny Reflexes and so on. These naming differences are for narrative purposes only: each is equivalent to the Quick Reflexes ability.
