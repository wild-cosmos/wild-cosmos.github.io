When this model arrives from reserve, they may be set up anywhere on the battlefield (including within 1" of enemy models − this is not treated as a charge). The model’s Mv value is reduced to 0" until the end of the phase, but they are treated as having moved their full ordinary Mv value for all purposes (for example, they cannot aim when attacking with ranged weapons).

Arriving via Deep Strike can be risky. After a model or unit has been set up using this ability, roll a D8: if the result is equal to or greater than the number given in brackets next to the ability, the model or unit is set up without incident. Otherwise, they have been displaced: your opponents roll off, and the winner may place them anywhere on the battlefield that is within 9" horizontally and 3" vertically of the position they were set up. Units must be placed in unit coherency.

If the result is a 1, the models have met an accident on arrival, and each of them suffers D4-1 injuries, as well as being displaced. If the model, or any models in the unit, were set up within 1" of any enemy models, or within difficult or dangerous terrain, they suffer these injuries on any failed roll (if they are only within 1" of enemy models or difficult or dangerous terrain after being displaced, they ignore this additional penalty).

Various ‘Strike’ abilities appear on different datasheets: Aerial Strike, Teleport Strike, Tunnelling Strike and so on. These naming differences are for narrative purposes only: each is equivalent to the Deep Strike ability.
