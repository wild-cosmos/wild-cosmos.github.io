Once each round, this model may attempt to heal a single friendly model within 3′′ (excluding Machines). The models that this model can heal are given in brackets next to this ability. When this model uses their Heal ability, nominate an eligible model to heal and roll a D8: if the value rolled is greater than or equal to the value given in brackets next to this ability, the nominated model regains 1 life.

If the nominated model has any Poison or Plague tokens, and this model makes a successful roll for their Heal ability, then instead of regaining life, the nominated model may instead choose to discard all of these tokens.

A model may use their Heal ability either in the Recovery step of the Resolution Phase, or at any time when a model with 0 life remaining is about to be destroyed, to prevent that model's destruction. They cannot use their Heal ability if they themselves have 0 life remaining.
