When you create your warband, if it includes this model, write down four abilities chosen from the list below. At the beginning of the game, before establishing the priority order, randomly select one of the abilities you chose − this model has that ability for the remainder of the game.

  • Arrogance of Aeons: While this model is on the battlefield, friendly Necrons do not make morale checks.
  • Enduring Will: This model adds 2 to their Lf value.
  • Hyperlogical Strategist: During deployment, when you set up a Necron model in your warband in reserve, you may roll two dice to determine when in the battle they can arrive from, and discard the highest result.
  • Immortal Pride: This model cannot panic, and they ignore the penalties for being seriously injured.
  • Implacable Conqueror: This model, and any Necron models in their unit, are always scoring models.
  • Maniacal Fury: When this model makes a melee attack, they add 1 to the A value of their primary weapon. If they charged in the previous Manoeuvring Phase, they add 2 to the A value of their primary weapon instead.
  • Martial Apotheosis: This model improves their BS or CS value by 1.
  • Merciless Aggressor: This model fights first in the Close Quarters Phase.
